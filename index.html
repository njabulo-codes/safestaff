ISO 45001 Compliance Monitor
—
—
Overall Score
Live conformity against monitored ISO 45001:2018 clauses
Methodology. Each clause is scored 0–100 from live operational data — percentages map directly; count-based indicators scale linearly to the stated target. Clause score = mean of its sub-metrics; overall score = mean of clauses with data. Bands: ≥85 Conformant · 60–84 Partial · <60 Attention. Scores are a management self-assessment aid, not a substitute for external certification audit. Full formula specification is documented in the technical header of this file.
